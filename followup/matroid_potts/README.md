# Graphic Shadows of Expanded Bipartite Hypergraphs

This directory is the canonical, review-oriented source for the follow-up paper
on Berge-cycle matroids, Tutte--Potts reductions, and monochromatic dependence.

Read the parts in order:

1. [`00_structure_matroid_polytope.md`](00_structure_matroid_polytope.md) —
   structural setting, canonical matroid, rank formula, polymatroid, and
   base-polytope translation;
2. [`01_coloring_tutte.md`](01_coloring_tutte.md) — weak and strong coloring,
   Potts reduction, closed forms, and hypergraph-Tutte specialization;
3. [`02_dependence_limits_ising.md`](02_dependence_limits_ising.md) — complete
   monochromatic-edge process, factorial moments, high-girth limits, and the
   Property-B/Ising reduction;
4. [`03_verification_literature_audit.md`](03_verification_literature_audit.md)
   — shadow uniqueness, computational checks, literature boundary, and audit
   map.

The bibliography is
[`MATROID_POTTS_FOLLOWUP_REFERENCES.bib`](../../MATROID_POTTS_FOLLOWUP_REFERENCES.bib).

These files consolidate the detailed mathematics from PRs #27 and #28 and add
the base-polytope translation theorem. They deliberately remain separate from
the Problem 593 manuscript and from the all-uniformity avoidance proof draft.
