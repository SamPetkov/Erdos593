Exact dependency and proof boundary
----------------------------------
kernel.tex, Lemma lem:rank-layer, is required for all subsets B of the 66
source incidences and all incidence sign assignments, uniformly over this
finite collection. The signed rank-r indicator is normalized by its exact
mass among symmetric D by D matrices, D=2r. The limiting model has independent
signs on the 33 point pairs. It is NOT enough to know the formula only for the
full incidence set or only for injective target-type assignments.

The proof separates into:
1. transverse.tex, Proposition prop:transverse-limit: the formula on the
   event that all 33 point differences are nonsingular; error O_D(q^-1/2).
2. tail.tex, Proposition prop:singular-tail: the unsigned normalized product
   on the complementary event is o(1).
3. orbits.tex, Lemma lem:chart-comparison: symmetric matrices form a dense
   Lagrangian chart, the unsigned normalizations are comparable, and graph
   intersections give the kernels of matrix differences.

The transverse computation alone is not a substitute for (2). Its local
uniform bounds are explicitly restricted to pairwise nonsingular point
differences. On singular configurations local factors can grow as powers of
q; equal point Lagrangians already give a negative local slack.

Transverse argument: independently checked steps
-----------------------------------------------
The rank-pair projection identity and maximal-isotropic parametrization are
consistent: rank A=rank(B-A)=r for nonsingular B of size 2r is equivalent to
A B^-1 A=A. The determinant sign relation contains the factor
chi((-1)^r) with the correct convention for A-B.

For a triple the substitution Q=B0^-1, L=B0^-1-B1^-1 makes Q uniform on
{Q: Q and Q-L nonsingular} conditional on L. The sign-match indicator stays
inside the expectation: the proof does not condition the restriction-sign
concentration on that indicator. This avoids a potentially serious misuse of
unconditional concentration. The local error is controlled in L1, while the
other transverse face factors are bounded uniformly, permitting a global
telescoping product without an independence assumption.

The joint determinant-sign argument uses the maximum point-pair degree six,
not independence of overlapping differences. With D>=12 its union bound for
nonzero linear combinations of the inverse lower-right blocks has a strict
negative exponent. The ensuing quadratic Gauss-sum bound gives total
variation O_D(q^-1/2) for the at most six Schur-complement values, and hence
for every nonempty moment of the 33 determinant signs. Finitely many moments
suffice for the asserted joint sign convergence. No defect found here.

Planted-law argument: independently checked steps
------------------------------------------------
The orbit ratio must use the mass of an actual ordered symplectic orbit,
not merely its intersection-dimension profile. The source does so. The
required lower bound is exactly Proposition prop:triple-orbits, audited by
the finite-structure referee.

Conditioning on deterministic C0 contained in the three point Lagrangians
leaves their quotients independent and uniform BEFORE mutual intersections
are imposed. The quotient identity
  (Li/C0) intersect Y' = ((Li intersect Y)+C0)/C0
is exact; no lifts are lost. The reduced center intersections Ri are therefore
independent uniform r'-spaces before their mutual profile is imposed.

The common-zero condition makes the three pair spaces Ri intersect Rj a
direct sum. The source's count may drop equality of the prospective span and
exactness of pair intersections because it needs an upper bound. Its exponent
delta P + sum p_i^2 + v w agrees with the elementary subspace count.

The lift forms are not asserted independent after taking their differences.
Lemma lem:planted-difference-image correctly identifies their joint image as
D1|V+D2|V+D3|V=0, of codimension b(dim V). A form prescribed on two subspaces
extends if its restrictions agree on their intersection; there is no third
compatibility condition for any one lift form. Conditioning independent
uniform forms on this image costs at most q^{b(v)}, yielding the stated
simultaneous-nullity upper bound.

The exponent maximum is over a fixed finite union of ACTUAL tuples at the
chosen D, across all odd fields. This is enough to make it independent of q.
No simultaneous realizability of the maximizing tuples on different faces
is needed in the subsequent upper bound.

Singular-tail argument: independently checked steps
---------------------------------------------------
The six exposure classes contain eleven faces covering thirteen points and
are connected through shared pairs. Therefore every parent-first spanning
tree order really does introduce one fresh point at every later face. The
conditional probability bound remains valid after all previous point
Lagrangians are exposed, by pair transitivity and independence of the fresh
point. The combination P(A)^6 <= product P(A_t) uses containment only, not
independence between the six overlapping class events.

I checked the exact slack decomposition, the quadratic/linear split, and the
two signs of delta in Lemma lem:coercivity. The scalar minima occur at the
displayed breakpoint or endpoint and give Q >= (P^2+T^2+delta^2)/342. The
coefficient of c really is nonnegative under the stated necessary dimension
constraints, so replacing c by |delta| is legitimate.

The propagation argument bounds all pair dimensions before choosing D; this
order is essential. Large pair dimension forces large common dimension on
every neighboring fully active face, and connectivity then forces a large
intersection at a pair with excess bit separation. This contradicts the
linear budget. Once pair dimensions are bounded, the coefficient
(c-u+v)/2 of d has the claimed nonnegative half-integral gap. The remaining
integer equality cases are exactly those described in the source: c=0 and
pair dimensions 0 or 2, after excluding the all-c=1 connected mode using
the excess-separation pair.

Lemma lem:tail-span is valid even for a partial linear dependence among pair
spaces: an inclusion-minimal dependent subfamily and one missing endpoint
give at least one additional local-span dimension. The nondirect residual
count permits an arbitrary ambient span and requires isotropy only locally.
The direct residual count imposes genuinely independent orthogonality
equations because all previously chosen basis vectors in that family are
linearly independent; mutual isotropy does not make the corresponding
linear functionals dependent. The cancellations leave exactly
-sum_e b(h_e). I checked the displayed finite constants 618, 1575, and 1641.

A quantitative clarification obtainable without new geometry
------------------------------------------------------------
The written source proof in fact supplies O_D(q^-1/6), rather than only o(1),
for the rank-layer error once D is fixed sufficiently large. The strict
first-cost gap is at least 1/6; direct residual and nondirect residual gaps
are at least 1; the transverse error is O_D(q^-1/2). The finite number of
profiles, signs, and activity sets makes this uniform over B and signs.
This is an extraction from the existing proof, not a new independent proof
of the rank-layer construction. The implied constant is not made effective
by this audit.

One can also make its dimension threshold explicit, very inefficiently.
The finite face-neighbor graph has diameter 5. Its separation frequencies
are: 28 pairs differ in one bit, four in two bits, and one in three bits.
Every bit class has eleven faces, covers all thirteen points, and is
connected; these facts were independently checked by a short exact script.

From the tail budget choose K=151, since
  (P+T)^2 <= 2*342*33*M < 151^2*M,
and the lower-activity bound is smaller. Set
  M_* = 4*(5+1)^2*151^2 = 3,283,344.
If M>M_*, propagation gives h_e>M/2 on every pair. Since at least one
a_e-1/3 >= 1/3, the excess is at least M^2/48; the budget is at most 33M,
contradicting M>1584. Thus this M_* is a valid universal bound.

In the source remainder R, 0<=c,p_i,t_i,v<=M_* and |delta|<=M_*. Using
|2p_i-t_i|<=2M_* gives |R|<=14 M_*^2 by termwise triangle inequalities.
Consequently the even dimension
  D_0 = M_* + 616 M_*^2 + 2
      = 6,640,694,261,842,322
satisfies every stated tail threshold, as well as D>1641 and D>=12.
Every even D>=D_0 therefore suffices for the source proof, with error
O_D(q^-1/6). This astronomical number is a consistency certificate for the
quantifier order, not a recommended addition to the paper or a practical
parameter choice. It should not be advertised as an optimized bound.

Attempts at simplifying the needed special case
-----------------------------------------------
It is tempting to use the small support of each corner activation law to
restrict the rank-layer theorem to at most seven active source incidences.
That is invalid. Source point and face type maps may repeat: mapping all
source points to a single target point type and all source faces to a
single incident target face type can activate the same coordinate on all
66 source incidences. Restricting to injective type maps also discards terms
that occur in the actual untyped homomorphism density.

Similarly, the proper-edge extraction in our Section 13 requires repeating
the finite coordinate expansion with source-incidence-dependent activation
laws. This is justified by its proof and the uniform all-B rank-layer input,
but is not obtained by literally substituting into a lemma whose statement
requires laws to depend only on target-type pairs.

No materially shorter independent substitute for the singular-tail bound
was obtained. A defensible narrower input is the unsigned weighted singular
tail proposition together with the separately checked transverse formula;
this makes the analytic bottleneck explicit but does not reduce the amount
of mathematics needed. The optional quantitative rate above is established
by the written estimates. Any claim of a substantially smaller admissible D,
or of a simpler restricted activation theorem, remains unproved here.
