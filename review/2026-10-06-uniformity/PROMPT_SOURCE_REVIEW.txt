4. SHARP MATCHING COEFFICIENT AND MATCHING-POLYTOPE PRIOR ART

(a) Jack Edmonds, Maximum matching and a polyhedron with 0,1-vertices,
J. Research National Bureau of Standards B69 (1965),125-130.
DOI 10.6028/jres.069B.013.
Primary full paper:
https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/jres/69B/jresv69Bn1-2p125_A1b.pdf
Section2, Theorem(P), explicitly describes the convex hull of matchings by
nonnegativity, degree sums at most1, and odd-set internal sums at most(|S|-1)/2.
This is the essential classical polyhedral input, not a new theorem here.

(b) Steffen Hoernig, On the minimum correlation between symmetrically distributed
random variables, Operations Research Letters46(4)(2018),469-471.
DOI 10.1016/j.orl.2018.06.005.
Primary institutional metadata and accepted-manuscript link:
https://novaresearch.unl.pt/en/publications/on-the-minimum-correlation-between-symmetrically-distributed-rand/
Working-paper full text (retrieved earlier; later access intermittently challenged):
https://run.unl.pt/bitstream/10362/82727/1/WP626.pdf
Proposition1 gives the exact minimum common correlation for exchangeable Bernoulli
variables. At success probability1/2 it is -1/(n-1) for even n and -1/n for odd n.
Therefore the parity obstruction to the constant matching coefficient is established
prior art. Cite this connection rather than advertising the binary bound as new.

(c) Rene Allerstorfer, Matthias Christandl, Dmitry Grinko, Ion Nechita, Maris Ozols,
Denis Rochette, Philip Verduyn Lunel, Monogamy of Highly Symmetric States,
Communications in Mathematical Physics407(1)(2026),Article21.
DOI 10.1007/s00220-025-05483-3. Online8December2025.
Primary full published PDF:
https://pure.uva.nl/ws/files/298742418/Monogamy_of_Highly_Symmetric_States.pdf
Lemma4.4, printed pp19-20, equations78-83, averages products over perfect matchings
(even n) and near-perfect matchings (odd n), obtaining exactly1/(n-1) and1/n as
mixture coefficients of maximally entangled and maximally mixed pair states.
This is a close quantum analogue of the matching-average construction. It is not
the universal classical statement for arbitrary arrays of prescribed pair kernels.
Their Theorem4.2 concerns special quantum isotropic states and has dimension-dependent
exceptions, so it must not be asserted as equivalent to the proposed theorem.

(d) Related searches checked finite exchangeability, pair-copula compatibility,
matching-polytopes and joint distributions. Konstantopoulos--Yuan,
On the extendibility of finitely exchangeable probability measures,
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.06188, gives general functional extension criteria;
no matching-polytope characterization located. Hurlimann's2014
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/2195-5832-1-7 concerns realizability of
trivariate rank-correlation matrices, not arbitrary complete pair distributions.
These are adjacent frameworks, not demonstrated competing results.

Bounded conclusion: I did not locate the exact universal iff characterization
that ALL finite bistochastic pair kernels admit jointly diluted marginals iff
lambda lies in the matching polytope. This is a bounded-search nonfinding, not
certified priority. The distinct claim is the full universal compatibility region,
with arbitrary edge-dependent kernels/finite spaces; sufficiency is the familiar
matching-mixture construction plus Edmonds, while necessity uses both binary
odd-set tests and large-alphabet involution tests for degrees. Cite the classical
inputs explicitly. Do not present matching averaging, parity bounds, or Edmonds'
polytope as new.

5. HIGH-UNIFORMITY DENSITY ESTIMATES: DIRECT COMPARISON

Jiaxi Nie and Sam Spiro, Sidorenko hypergraphs and random Turan numbers,
Combinatorial Theory5(4)(2025),#10, DOI10.5070/C65465676.
Primary published paper:
https://escholarship.org/uc/item/0c25t471
https://escholarship.org/content/qt0c25t471/qt0c25t471.pdf
Local complete PDF/text: literature_scan/nie_spiro_2025_published.pdf and
literature_scan/nie_spiro_published.txt.
Their Theorem1.13 already implies s(E^r(F))=O_F(1/r) when s(F)<infinity, hence
fixed-positive-density one-sided asymptotic Sidorenko behavior. For general graph
F, applying the result after a sufficiently high partite expansion gives the
analogous qualitative implication; this is our deduction, not their literal
statement. Their theorem does not state the proposed explicit two-sided
m(m-1)p(1-p)/r bound or the regular, girth-sensitive O_F(r^-g) bound.

Wassily Hoeffding, A class of statistics with asymptotically normal distribution,
Annals of Mathematical Statistics19(3)(1948),293-325,
DOI10.1214/aoms/1177730196, is the standard projection/decomposition source.
A recent nearby use is Heng Li and Xizhi Liu, Forcing quasirandomness via rooted
F-densities, arXiv2608.08679v1:
https://arxiv.org/html/2608.08679v1
Section4, particularly Proposition4.1 and the following symmetric-hyperkernel
variance calculation, applies Hoeffding components to rooted rigidity/stability.
No private-expansion high-uniformity limit statement was located in its full text.
It is methodological overlap, not a superseding theorem found by this audit.

Targeted searches for expansion densities, uniformity, regular kernels, girth,
Hoeffding projections, and corresponding matching compatibility did not locate
the exact new quantitative assertions. The useful bounded distinction is:
  - already known: one-sided qualitative approximation from Nie--Spiro;
  - standard tools: Hoeffding orthogonality, leaf cancellation, cycle operator bounds;
  - proposed additional content: explicit two-sided bounds uniform over all bounded
    symmetric r-kernels, regular girth-sensitive rate, and sharp cycle examples at
    density bounded away from zero.
This report does not certify first priority or substitute for independent proof
checking. The new results are analytic arguments, not covered by family161's Lean
challenge.
