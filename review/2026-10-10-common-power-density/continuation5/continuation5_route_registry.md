## Open obligation 1: averaged actual unequal-star projection contraction

Let `S` be any actual finite nonnegative self-adjoint Markov root on
original positive probability `pi`, `B=S^2`, and `Q=Q*=Q^2` any
original-law projection of rank `d`. With the **actual** sources

\[
R_t=QD_{\mathbf1_{\{t\}}/\pi_t}Q\big|_{\operatorname{ran}Q},
\]

the outstanding statement is

\[
\boxed{\mathbb E_{t\sim\pi}\operatorname{tr}
 [(B^xR)_t(B^yR)_t(B^zR)_t]\ge d
 \quad\text{for all positive integers }x,y,z.}
\tag{U}
\]

It is proved here for three independent common-cone kernels, and the
quadratic lower estimate supplies further certifiable instances. The
pointwise version and the uniform fractional-surplus strengthening are
false. Statement (U) itself is neither proved nor refuted. It is not
counted as progress merely to restate it.

If proved, (U) and the existing quadrilateral lemma would extend the
quantitative comparison to every binary projection channel with actual
coarse powers. That alone would not resolve arbitrary actual roots of the
original target, nor would it prove every coarse density. No reduction to
this channel class is being assumed.

## Open obligation 2: collective all-sheet common-power cover comparison

For an actual target root `T` on original `mu`, write its six powers as
`K_(n_e)`. For every sheet number `M>=1` and six permutations
`sigma_e in Sym(M)`, define the actual cover partition function

\[
Z_\sigma=
\mathbb E_{\mu^{4M}}
\prod_{e=vw\in E(K_4)}\prod_{a=1}^M
K_{n_e}(x_{v,a},x_{w,\sigma_e(a)}).
\]

The remaining sufficient comparison is

\[
\boxed{Z_\sigma\le F(T;n)^M
\quad\text{for every }M\text{ and every }\sigma.}
\tag{C}
\]

The [parent packet's original-law type/entropy argument](../route-registry.md)
proves `limsup_M (E_sigma Z_sigma)^(1/M)>=1`. Therefore (C) would imply
the exact target. It compares different finite graphs and is stronger
than the target; it is not the supplied Dirichlet identity rearranged.

The new actual examples block coefficientwise approaches to (C), not
(C) itself. Their complete covers stay below `F^2`. The barbell Gram rule
shows a legitimate kind of collective compensation, but no general
availability of its needed coefficient matrix is asserted. No implication
from checking seven two-covers to all sheet numbers is assumed. A future
counterexample to (C) alone would still not prove `F<1`.

These two obligations have different mechanisms and scopes: (U) is an
actual multiplication-field inequality advancing a restricted transfer;
(C) is a global finite-cover comparison sufficient for the target. No
additional theorem-strength condition is hidden as a routine completion
step.
