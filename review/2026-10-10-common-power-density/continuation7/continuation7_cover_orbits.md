# Simultaneous cover comparisons from exact character-flow budgets

## Verdict and scope

The unrestricted density inequality and the all-cover comparison (C)
remain unresolved. This note proves a simultaneous-cover extension on the
**same actual 32-state host supplied in the question**. For every integer
`k>=4`, every sheet count, and every cover whose connected components each
have covering degree at most three, the complete cover partition function
is at most the corresponding power of the base density. The theorem
includes genuinely noncommuting three-sheet permutations. It does not
assume that they reduce to one permuted edge.

The result follows from 48 exact polynomial certificates: seven connected
two-sheet types and 41 connected three-sheet types. Of the latter, 28 have
noncommuting chord permutations. The certificates hold on the entire
parameter interval needed for `k>=4`, not just at a finite list of tuples.
Negative mode assignments and negative defect coefficients are retained.
No positivity of three PSD matrix factors, real-spectrum claim for a
general transfer, or inference from numerical matching is used.

The host's density was already covered in earlier work. This is new cover
scope inside the current programme, not a new host-density class, an
unrestricted theorem, or a literature novelty assertion. Arbitrarily large
**connected** covers are outside the theorem.

The companion `continuation7_cover_orbits_verify.py` and generated
`continuation7_cover_orbits_checks.json` give the complete finite rational
certificate. The proof below explains every enumeration and the original
measure transport on which it depends.

## 1. The actual host and the cover partition function

Use the edge order `ab,ac,ad,bc,bd,cd`. For six signs `R_e`, let

\[
S_a=R_{ab}R_{ac}R_{ad},\quad S_b=R_{ab}R_{bc}R_{bd},\quad
S_c=R_{ac}R_{bc}R_{cd},\quad S_d=R_{ad}R_{bd}R_{cd}.
\]

The original state space is the 32 sign assignments satisfying
`S_a+S_b+S_c-S_d=2`, each with original probability `1/32`. Put

\[
s=(2^{-64},2^{-5},2^{-2},2^{-3},2^{-4},2^{-3}),\qquad
T(x,y)=1+\sum_{i=1}^{6}s_iR_i(x)R_i(y),
\]
\[
p=(51/32+2^{-64})^{-1},\qquad W=pT.                 \tag{1}
\]

Direct original-law moments give centered orthonormal `R_i`. Thus `T` is
a self-adjoint Markov operator and

\[
T(x,y)\ge13/32-2^{-64}>0,\qquad
\max T=51/32+2^{-64}=p^{-1}.
\]

Consequently `W` is an actual admissible strictly positive host. All square
powers are

\[
K_j(x,y)=1+\sum_{i=1}^{6}2^{-d_i j}R_i(x)R_i(y),\qquad
(d_1,\ldots,d_6)=(128,10,4,6,8,6).                   \tag{2}
\]

The entire centered square spectrum has five distinct positive values and
the additional zero value, of multiplicity 25. It is not a two-value
whole-centered example.

The tuple is always

\[
(k,u,r,l,h)=(k,4,1,1,8),\qquad k\ge4,
\]

so the six square powers are `(k,9,4,1,4,1)`. Let `F(k)` be its original
four-variable density. For `M` sheets and six permutations `sigma_e`,

\[
Z_\sigma(k)=\mathbb E_{\mu^{4M}}
\prod_{e=vw}\prod_{s=1}^{M}
K_{n_e}(x_{v,s},x_{w,\sigma_e(s)}).                \tag{3}
\]

Every variable in (3) has the original law `mu`; no sheet-conditioned host
law or component normalization is introduced.

## 2. Gauge transport and factorization under the unchanged original law

Relabel the sheets separately at each of the four base vertices. If
`x_(v,g_v(s))` is renamed `y_(v,s)`, the transformed edge permutation is

\[
\sigma'_{vw}=g_w^{-1}\sigma_{vw}g_v.               \tag{4}
\]

This is a permutation of the actual integration variables and preserves
the original product measure exactly. Choose

`g_a=id`, `g_b=sigma_ab`, `g_c=sigma_ac`, `g_d=sigma_ad`.

The three tree-edge permutations become identities. The remaining chord
permutations on `bc,bd,cd` are denoted `alpha,beta,gamma`.

Let `H` be the subgroup of `Sym(M)` generated by these three permutations.
For every `H`-orbit `O`, the vertices `v x O` form a connected component of
the cover; conversely every connected component is of this form. Indeed,
the identity tree paths identify the sheet labels at all four vertex
types, and a chord followed by a tree return realizes its permutation and
inverse. Thus connectedness is exactly membership in an `H`-orbit.

Different components use disjoint integration variables and have no edge
between them. Product-measure integration therefore gives

\[
\boxed{Z_\sigma(k)=\prod_{O} Z_{\sigma|O}(k).}      \tag{5}
\]

The factor for a component of covering degree `m=|O|` is an integral under
`mu^(4m)` with the *same* `mu` as (3). There is no probability distribution
on the set of sheets to renormalize. A singleton orbit contributes exactly
`F(k)`.

The theorem's restriction is `|O|<=3` for every orbit. It is invariant under
the choice of sheet labels and is equivalent to saying that every
connected component has covering degree at most three. The total number
of sheets need not be bounded, and the global permutation subgroup need
not be commutative.

## 3. Original cubic moments become a finite character-flow problem

Write `phi_0=1` and `phi_i=R_i`. Assign the seven modes the characters

\[
c=(0,3,5,6,1,2,4)\subset\mathbb F_2^3,\qquad c_0=0,
\tag{6}
\]

using three-bit integers and bitwise exclusive-or for addition. The one
omitted character is seven. Direct integration over the 32 original states
gives, for every triple of modes,

\[
M_{ijk}=\mathbb E_\mu\phi_i\phi_j\phi_k=
\begin{cases}
0,&c_i+c_j+c_k\ne0,\\
1,&c_i+c_j+c_k=0\text{ and one character is zero},\\
-1/2,&\{c_i,c_j,c_k\}=\{2,4,6\},\\
1/2,&\text{otherwise, when the three nonzero characters sum to zero}.
\end{cases}                                                    \tag{7}
\]

There are 43 nonzero ordered cubic moments. The verifier evaluates all
343 original sums and verifies (7), the orthonormality relations, all
original row sums, and `T^2=K_1` by rational matrix multiplication.

Expand every actual factor (2) in a connected cover of degree `m`. Each
edge receives one of the seven modes. Integrating an actual degree-three
vertex contributes its moment (7). Thus only assignments whose three
incident characters sum to zero survive. This is an exact character-flow
constraint, not an assumption that the original sources are arbitrary.

Fix a spanning tree of the connected lifted graph. There are `4m` vertices
and `6m` edges, hence exactly `2m+1` non-tree edges. Give each non-tree edge
one of the seven allowed characters. Starting from tree leaves, the flow
constraint determines its last incident tree-edge character uniquely.
Reject the assignment if it is the omitted character seven. At the root,
the constraint is automatic because each edge contributes twice to the
sum of all vertex boundaries.

Every surviving flow is obtained once and only once. The finite search
therefore has `7^(2m+1)` candidates. In particular the connected two-sheet
and three-sheet certificates need only `7^5=16807` and `7^7=823543`
candidates each, respectively. This replaces the much larger independent
choice of all `6m` edge modes without dropping any terms.

## 4. Exact one-variable polynomials and a collective interval budget

Put `z=2^-k` and set `d_0=0`. Every factor on an `ab` lift contributes
`z^(d_i)`; the other five edge types have the fixed powers `(9,4,1,4,1)`.
For a surviving flow `a`, let

\[
q(a)=\sum_{e\text{ over }ab}d_{a_e},\quad
D(a)=\sum_{e\text{ not over }ab}n_e d_{a_e}+b(a),          \tag{8}
\]

where `b(a)` counts vertices whose three incident characters are nonzero.
Let `t(a)` count vertices whose character triple is `{2,4,6}`. Equations
(2) and (7), using the original product integration, give

\[
\boxed{P_\sigma(z)=\sum_{a\text{ a surviving flow}}
(-1)^{t(a)}2^{-D(a)}z^{q(a)},\qquad
Z_\sigma(k)=P_\sigma(2^{-k}).}                         \tag{9}
\]

This is an exact finite identity. The denominator exponent is at most

`128(9+4+1+4+1)m+4m=2436m`.

The implementation accumulates the coefficient numerator after multiplying
every term by `2^(2436m)`. Its character-flow accumulation uses integer
shifts and additions; coefficient fractions are formed only afterward.
Negative terms are included with their actual signs.

Let `P(z)` be the base polynomial computed with `m=1`, and let

\[
\Delta_\sigma(z)=P(z)^m-P_\sigma(z)
=\sum_q a_q z^q.                                      \tag{10}
\]

An independent enumeration of all `7^6` base edge-mode assignments checks
the base polynomial and its 235 surviving terms. To certify (10) on the
whole required interval `0<z<=1/16`, let `q_0` be its least nonzero degree.
Then

\[
\frac{\Delta_\sigma(z)}{z^{q_0}}
\ge a_{q_0}+
\sum_{q>q_0}\min(a_q,0)\,2^{-4(q-q_0)}=:b_\sigma.       \tag{11}
\]

Positive higher coefficients can only increase the expression. For a
negative coefficient, `z^(q-q_0)<=2^(-4(q-q_0))` supplies a lower bound
with the correct reversed direction. Thus (11) is a collective polynomial
budget that preserves every negative coefficient; it does not ask for
coefficientwise positivity.

## 5. All connected types through degree three

After tree gauge fixing, simultaneous conjugation of the three chord
permutations is a common sheet relabeling and preserves the integral. All
triples are enumerated, and the lexicographically least conjugate is used
as representative.

For two sheets the group is abelian and there are eight triples: the
identity triple and seven connected types. For three sheets, Burnside's
formula gives

\[
\frac{6^3+3\cdot2^3+2\cdot3^3}{6}=49
\]

simultaneous-conjugacy classes. Exactly eight are disconnected: the
identity triple and the seven nontrivial triples in a common transposition
subgroup. Hence there are 41 connected types. Thirteen are commuting:
the 26 nonidentity triples in the order-three subgroup pair under
inversion. The other 28 generate the noncommutative group `S_3`.

For **all 48 connected representatives** the exact coefficients in the
JSON certificate verify

\[
\boxed{q_0\in\{0,4\},\qquad b_\sigma>2^{-60}.}          \tag{12}
\]

The finite certificate lists the three actual chord permutations, every
nonzero coefficient of (10), the full rational number (11), the count of
negative coefficients, and the flow counts for each representative. Thus
each of the finite inequalities in (12) can be checked either from its
listed coefficients or directly from (7)--(11). The program exhaustively
checks the classification, the 28 noncommuting types, and all 48 rational
inequalities. It is a deterministic certificate, with no tolerances.
To keep the complete table reasonably sized, an encoded dyadic rational
`[h,d]` means exactly `int(h,16)/2^d`, with a signed hexadecimal numerator.
The conversion is lossless and does not round the listed coefficients or
interval bounds.

Six of the seven two-sheet types and six of the 41 three-sheet types are
gauge-equivalent to a single permuted edge and were already covered by
continuation6. The remaining **36 connected types** are genuinely
simultaneous: the bipartite double cover and 35 three-sheet types, including
all 28 noncommuting types. The verifier identifies this overlap explicitly;
the preceding one-edge theorem is not counted as a newly proved case.

Because `0<z<=1/16<1`, (11)--(12) prove the uniform comparison

\[
\boxed{
Z_\sigma(k)<F(k)^m-\delta(k),\qquad
\delta(k)=2^{-4k-60},\quad m\in\{2,3\}.
}                                                        \tag{13}
\]

This includes the bipartite double cover, which is not gauge-equivalent
to a single permuted base edge, and every connected three-sheet cover,
including all 28 noncommuting types. No passage from a handful of numeric
values to (13) is used: the rational interval bound proves all `k>=4`.

## 6. Arbitrary sheet counts with the stated component restriction

Combine (5) and (13). If `q` is the number of nonsingleton monodromy orbits,
then

\[
\boxed{
Z_\sigma(k)
\le F(k)^M
\prod_{|O|\ge2}
\left(1-\frac{\delta(k)}{F(k)^{|O|}}\right)
\le F(k)^M.
}                                                        \tag{14}
\]

The factors are nonnegative because each actual component partition
function is positive and its defect is larger than `delta(k)`. If any
component has degree two or three, the final comparison is strict. If all
orbits are singletons, the cover is isomorphic over the labeled base graph
to disjoint copies, and equality is exactly `Z=F^M`.

This establishes the claimed simultaneous-cover family. It does not
control connected covers of degree four or larger. The character-flow
identity still holds in those degrees, but neither the finite check of
(12) nor its lower bound has been proved uniformly as the degree grows.
That unproved uniformity is a substantive remaining part of (C), not a
routine compatibility step. A counterexample to (C) would also have to be
distinguished from a counterexample to the requested density inequality.

## 7. Diagnostic search record

Before the exact sign-host calculation, a bounded deterministic scan used
36 symmetric nonnegative integer-flow roots on original dimensions
3, 4, and 5. Six fixed admissible tuples and all seven complete nontrivial
two-cover gauges gave 1512 floating-point comparisons. No ratio exceeded
`1+10^-8`; the largest recorded value was `1+4*10^-15`, within rounding
error at a near-equality input. This is not a proof and is not an input to
any assertion above.

The scripts and saved records identify the original probabilities by
`mu_i=sum_j G_ij/sum_ij G_ij` and the actual root by
`T_ij=(sum_ij G_ij)G_ij/(sum_j G_ij)(sum_j G_ji)`. Thus the scan did not
substitute arbitrary PSD kernels. Its role was to test the complete cover
quantity before choosing a finite character-flow certificate. No local
optimizer, global-search guarantee, or target counterexample is claimed.
